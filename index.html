Watch My Calories

Snap, track, and stay fit with AI.

Take a photo of your meal, or of a restaurant menu, and Watch My Calories estimates calories, protein, carbs, and fat for every item. Track your day on the Dashboard and see your trends in History.

  • AI food and menu analysis
  • Daily calorie goal, adjusted for activity from Apple Health
  • 7- and 30-day calorie trends, days on target, and logging streak
  • No account needed — your data stays on your device
